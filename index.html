Where Did My Complaint Go?

Designing transparency into the hostel maintenance complaint process • Author: Aryaman Saboo

Core Problem Space

Hostel residents face anxiety not primarily due to slow repairs, but because of zero visibility into what happens after submitting a complaint. VUFIX addresses this by implementing an interactive live timeline, clear accountability, and student resolution confirmation.
