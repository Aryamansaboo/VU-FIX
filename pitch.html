02
The Problem

Hostel complaints disappear into thin air

Verbal / Paper Complaints
No record, no ticket ID, no proof of submission.
Zero Visibility
Students couldn't see status, assigned technician, or ETA.
14+ Day Resolution Times
Without tracking, follow-ups were manual, causing huge delays.
Warden Overload
Manual routing to technicians was error-prone and slow.
14+
days avg. resolution without a system
"I reported a broken fan and had no idea who was handling it. I went to the warden's office 3 times."— Hostel A Student (User Interview)
03
The Solution

VUFIX — One system, full transparency

A browser-based complaint management platform with real-time tracking, automated technician assignment, and role-based dashboards.

Instant Ticketing

3-step wizard creates a tracked ticket in <60 seconds with auto-generated ID.

Auto-Assignment

Issue category auto-matches the right specialist — Electrical, Plumbing, IT, etc.

Live Timeline

Every status update is logged with timestamps. Students see exactly where things stand.

Warden Dashboard

Warden Rinu Babu manages all tickets, assigns staff, sets ETAs from one control panel.

Escalation System

Students can escalate stalled complaints. Warden gets an immediate alert banner.

Resolution Confirmation

Students verify repairs and rate service quality — closing the feedback loop.

Built for real-world use, not demos

Submitted → Under Review → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed — each step timestamped with responsible person.
Escalation System
Student flags → warden gets alert banner

Tickets become urgency-class with blinking red badge. Warden alert banner persists until resolved.

Student Resolution Confirmation
Closes feedback loop with 5-star rating

Warden marks "Resolved" → student confirms → rates 1–5 stars → ticket officially closes.

Zero-Backend Architecture
localStorage persistence, no server needed

Reactive state via VUFIXStore — all data persists in browser with auto-save.

localStorageReactive EventsNo FrameworkZero Dependencies
